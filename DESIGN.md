# Prototype interface
Operate-first booking interface; warm ivory #f6f3ec, forest #183d35, copper #9b5d3b, ink #202e2a. Serif headings and legible sans-serif body. Quiet editorial spacing, thin rules and broad sections. Mobile nav wraps without hidden essential actions. Forms have labels, keyboard focus, pending/error/empty states. The prototype does not claim to reproduce an unseen Figma file.
